# Chapter 5: AI Decision Making & Learning

This chapter explores how artificial intelligence enables robots to make decisions and learn from experience.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Understand different AI approaches for robotic decision making
- Explain reinforcement learning in robotics
- Describe neural networks and their applications
- Analyze learning algorithms for robotic systems

## Introduction to AI Decision Making

AI decision making in robotics involves algorithms that enable robots to choose actions based on their goals, environment, and past experiences. This includes planning, reasoning, and adaptation capabilities.

## Machine Learning in Robotics

Machine learning enables robots to improve their performance through experience. Key approaches include:

1. **Supervised Learning**: Learning from labeled examples
2. **Unsupervised Learning**: Finding patterns in unlabeled data
3. **Reinforcement Learning**: Learning through rewards and punishments
4. **Deep Learning**: Using neural networks with multiple layers

## Neural Networks

Neural networks are computing systems inspired by the human brain. They are particularly effective for pattern recognition, control, and decision making in complex environments.

## Autonomous Behavior

AI enables robots to exhibit autonomous behavior, making decisions without direct human intervention while adapting to changing conditions.

## Challenges and Future Directions

Current challenges include real-time processing, safety, and generalization across different environments and tasks.
