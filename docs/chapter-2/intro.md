# Chapter 2: Humanoid Robotics Fundamentals

This chapter covers the fundamental concepts of humanoid robotics - the design, construction, and control of robots that resemble the human body structure.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this chapter, you will be able to:
- Understand the principles of humanoid robot design
- Explain kinematics and dynamics in humanoid robots
- Identify different types of humanoid robot configurations
- Analyze the challenges in humanoid robotics

## What are Humanoid Robots?

Humanoid robots are robots that are built to resemble the human body. They typically have a head, a torso, two arms, and two legs, though some variations exist. The human-like form factor allows them to interact with environments and tools designed for humans.

## Key Components of Humanoid Robots

Humanoid robots consist of several key components:

1. **Mechanical Structure**: The physical body including joints and limbs
2. **Actuators**: Motors and mechanisms that enable movement
3. **Sensors**: Systems that provide environmental awareness
4. **Control Systems**: Algorithms that coordinate movement and behavior
5. **Power Systems**: Energy sources and distribution

## Applications of Humanoid Robotics

Humanoid robots have applications in:
- Service and assistance
- Research and development
- Entertainment
- Education
- Healthcare support
